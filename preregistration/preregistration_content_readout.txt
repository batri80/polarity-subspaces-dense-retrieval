Semantic Odometry: pre-registration of the content-readout test
Recorded: 27 October 2026, before any content-readout result was computed.
Author: Batri Krishnan

Background
SciFact counts abstracts that support OR refute a claim as relevant, so its
relevance is polarity-blind. After training on NevIR (polarity-matching), the
SciFact nDCG@10 drop from zero-shot E5 (seed 1) was concentrated in claims
containing explicit negation (plain_ft -0.28, frame -0.43, n = 35) relative to
affirmative claims (-0.12, -0.18, n = 265). Affirmative claims, however,
still account for most of the total loss.

Hypothesis
The frame subspace U carries orientation (polarity). Removing it yields a
polarity-blind "aboutness" score suited to topical retrieval, while the full
readout retains orientation for negation and exclusion tasks. This is the
content/frame separation proposed in the original Semantic Odometry paradigm.

Fixed protocol (no tuning; decided before results)
Content readout: both query and document embedded as normalize((I - U U^T) h),
where h is the encoder's pooled output before any frame transport.
U: the model's own frame subspace (learned U for frame models; the fixed U for
frame_randomU; for models without a frame, U refitted by the probe procedure on
that model's own NevIR training pairs). Rank k = 8.
Control: the identical projection with a random orthonormal U of rank 8.
Evaluated: SciFact nDCG@10 (overall, negated claims, affirmative claims),
NevIR test pairwise accuracy, ExcluIR pairwise RR. Three seeds.

Predictions
C1. For the frame model, the content readout raises SciFact nDCG@10 above its
    full readout, with the larger recovery on negated claims.
C2. The random-U projection recovers clearly less than the model's own U.
C3. The content readout lowers NevIR accuracy relative to the full readout
    (orientation removed), which confirms that U carries polarity.
C4 (secondary). For plain_ft, projecting out its refitted negation subspace
    recovers SciFact less than for the frame model, because plain fine-tuning
    does not concentrate polarity in a known subspace.
Support for the hypothesis requires C1 and C2; C3 is the mechanism check.
A result in which the content readout does not recover SciFact will be
reported as a negative result. The aggregate recovery is expected to be
partial, because most of the SciFact loss lies in affirmative claims.
